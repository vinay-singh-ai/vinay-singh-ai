## 🧠 What I'm Building

> **AI hallucinates. In law, finance, and government — that's not a bug. That's a liability.**

I'm the **Founder & CEO of AVYON Technologies**, building **TruthChat** — an enterprise-grade Agentic RAG platform that eliminates hallucinations through **Verifier-Gated Decoding**, cyclic multi-agent reasoning, and **SHA-256 cryptographic provenance manifests**.

Most AI systems are impressive demos. TruthChat is infrastructure — built for environments where being wrong has **legal consequences**.

```
Core Problem  →  LLMs fail silently in high-stakes, regulated environments
Core Solution →  Evidence-gated AI with cryptographic claim verification
Architecture  →  Cyclic multi-agent (LangGraph) + Hybrid RAG + Cross-Encoder
Benchmarks    →  Hallucination <1% · Recall@k ≥ 0.90 · 100% data lineage
Deployment    →  Air-gapped VPCs · Data sovereignty · Zero-trust ready
Market        →  Legal · Finance · Government (regulated enterprise AI)
```

> *"I build AI systems that are verifiable, not just capable. Most AI demos work. Most AI products fail in production. I work on the gap between those two things."*
